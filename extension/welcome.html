Guardian is now protecting this browser

Family Scam Guard

Why Chrome said "read and change all your data on all websites"

Scam messages can arrive on any website: WhatsApp Web, Gmail, Facebook and others. To warn you, Guardian has to look at the messages on the page you are reading.

  • It reads only on this computer. Guardian checks messages with simple rules on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere while it watches.
  • It never saves or shares your messages. It only remembers the phone numbers and links of scams you checked, on this computer, so it can warn you if they come back.
  • It only changes the page to warn you: a red outline around a risky message, and Guardian in the corner.

How Guardian works

  • 🟡 Watching: Guardian sits quietly in the bottom-right corner.
  • ⚠️ Be careful / 🚨 Scam: a message shows warning signs. Guardian reacts right away and shows why.
  • 🔵 Checking: press "Check with AI". The AI runs on your device first (Gemini Nano) and uses the cloud only if needed.
  • ✅ Safe: the AI found no scam signs and tells you why.

Setting it up for a parent?

Open the Family Scam Guard icon in the toolbar, go to Family, and enter a family code. When Guardian finds a risky message, you get an alert. You never see the message itself.
